# Rawframe trademark policy

"Rawframe", "Rawframe Engine", "Kest", "Maul" and their logos are trademarks of Sirac Ozmen. The licenses of the code do not grant rights to these names or logos. This policy says how you may use them.

## Uses that need no permission

- Saying truthfully that your game, tool or library uses, works with or is built on Rawframe, Kest or a Maul library: "Made with Rawframe Engine", "Scripted in Kest", "Uses Maul2D".
- Referring to the projects in articles, talks, tutorials, courses and reviews.
- Naming a community, meetup or channel about the projects, as long as it does not look official (for example "Kest users" rather than "Kest Official").
- Showing the unmodified logos next to such statements, at a size that does not dominate your own branding.

## Uses that need permission

- Using a name or logo in the name of your product, company, domain or package, such as "Rawframe Studio Pro" or "kest-official".
- Distributing a modified version of Rawframe Engine under the name "Rawframe". A modified build must be renamed, and may say it is "based on Rawframe Engine".
- Selling merchandise with the names or logos.
- Anything that suggests the project endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with you.

## Never allowed

- Using the names or logos in a way that is misleading or damages the projects' reputation.
- Altering the logos, or combining them with your own marks.
- Registering trademarks or domains that contain the names.

## Asking

Ask by email at support@rawframe.net, or by opening an issue in `Rawframe-Project/.github` with the subject "Trademark request". Most reasonable requests are answered quickly.
