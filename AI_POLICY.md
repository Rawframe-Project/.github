# AI policy

The Rawframe Project is developed with AI coding agents. Maintainers direct the agents, review what they produce, and are responsible for every change that lands. We say this openly because it is how the work is done.

The rules below apply to outside contributions.

- **Disclose AI use.** If an AI tool helped write an issue, a comment or code, say which tool and how much of the work it did.
- **Understand everything you submit.** You must be able to explain what your change does and how it interacts with the rest of the system without the help of an AI tool. If you cannot, do not submit it.
- **Edit what the AI wrote.** Issues and comments are read by people. Trim generated text to what matters, check every claim, and remove filler.
- **Verify before you submit.** Run the checks and the relevant tests yourself. Do not submit code you have not run.
- **No AI-generated media** such as images, audio or video in contributions.

Contributions that ignore these rules are closed without review.
